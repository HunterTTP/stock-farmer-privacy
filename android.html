Stock Farmer Privacy Policy

Stock Farmer for Android stores your farms and preferences on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, it also keeps a copy of your farms in your own Google account as Play Games Saved Games, so you can carry on on another device; Google holds that copy under your account, and the developer cannot see it. Each farm's save records the model of the device that last saved it (such as "Google Pixel 9"), so you can tell your devices apart; it stays in your saves on your devices and in your Play Games copy. The game does not require an account and does not send your farm, animal names, or settings to the developer.

Android Auto Backup may also copy your farms to your Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone or reinstall the game. You can turn it off in your device's Settings, under Google, Backup.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or photos. Its permissions are internet access, network state (to tell when you are offline), and notifications, plus three that WorkManager, the Android library that schedules the crop reminders, adds for its background work: run at startup (so reminders survive a restart), prevent the device from sleeping, and run a foreground service.

If you own stocks or open the Stock Market in your house, the app periodically (at most every five minutes while the Stock Market is open) asks Yahoo Finance for the current price of the tickers you search for and own. Those requests come from your device and contain only the ticker symbols, with the network information any internet request carries; your farm is never sent.

If you allow notifications, Stock Farmer schedules crop reminders locally on your device. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings. Music and sound effects can be turned off separately in the game's Settings.

You can delete a farm from Load Game, which also removes it from Play Games the next time the game syncs, unless another device still has it. Recovery copies of replaced saves remain in the app's local storage; removing the app deletes its local data. You can delete the Play Games copy in the Play Games app's settings, under Delete Play Games account and data, and the Auto Backup copy in your device's Settings, under Google, Backup.

The game keeps a short list of recent problems with Play Games, saving and stock prices on your device, for troubleshooting; it is never sent anywhere, is not backed up, and you can read, copy or clear it in Admin, Diagnostics. Any diagnostic information you choose to share through Google is governed by your device settings and Google's privacy practices.
