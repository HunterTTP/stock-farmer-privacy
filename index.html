Stock Farmer Privacy Policy

Stock Farmer stores your farms and preferences on your device. On iOS 26 and later, if you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, it also keeps a copy of your farms in your own iCloud account so you can carry on on another device; Apple holds that copy under your Apple Account, and the developer cannot see it. Each farm's save records the name of the device that last saved it (the name you type in Load Game, or the device's model), so you can tell your devices apart; it stays in your saves on your devices and in your iCloud. It does not require an account and does not send your farm, animal names, or settings to the developer.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services. It does not request access to your contacts, location, or microphone. If you choose Save Farm Photo, it asks for permission to add the farm picture to Photos. It does not read your existing photos.

If you own stocks or open the Stock Market in your house, the app periodically (at most every five minutes while the game is open) asks Yahoo Finance for the current price of the tickers you search for and own. Those requests come from your device and contain only the ticker symbols, with the network information any internet request carries; your farm is never sent.

If you allow notifications, Stock Farmer schedules crop reminders locally on your device. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings. Music and sound effects can be turned off separately in the game's Settings.

You can delete a farm from Load Game, which also removes it from iCloud the next time the game syncs, unless another device still has it. Recovery copies of replaced saves remain in the app's local storage; removing the app deletes its local data. You can remove the iCloud copy in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Account Storage. Your device's backup settings may retain app data in Apple-managed backups.

The game keeps a short list of recent problems with iCloud, saving and stock prices on your device, for troubleshooting; it is never sent anywhere. Any diagnostic information you choose to share through Apple is governed by your device settings and Apple's privacy practices.
